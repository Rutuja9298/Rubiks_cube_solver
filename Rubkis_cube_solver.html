Rubik's Cube — Simple demo & reverse-scramble "solver"

This demo implements an OOP cube representation, manual face rotations, scramble generation and an automatic solver that reverses the scramble moves (so it will always solve cubes scrambled by this app). The moveset uses standard face notation: U, D, L, R, F, B (with primes ' and 2).

Colors: W=white, R=red, G=green, Y=yellow, O=orange, B=blue
